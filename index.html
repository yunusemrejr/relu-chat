How it works

From your question to an answer, one step at a time

  1. 1

    Retrieve

    Keyword matching and field-weighted BM25 find candidate topics instantly. An optional ~22 MB sentence model adds broader wording matches.

  2. 2

    Read the signals

    Entities, intent cues and ranked scores merge into one structured packet, all on your device.

  3. 3

    Decide

    A 13,079-parameter policy network chooses how to respond: answer, clarify, compare or follow up.

  4. 4

    Compose

    Curated fragments are joined with connectors into a precise answer. Nothing is generated.

  5. 5

    Render

    KaTeX typesets the math and session memory keeps follow-ups in context.

About

An open-source experiment in simpler chatbots

ReLU.chat is built by Yunus Emre Vurgun to find simpler, faster ways to build chatbots, without creating an LLM or leaning on pre-LLM chatbot logic.

The goal is to combine sentence transformers, BM25 sparse retrieval, a reinforcement-learned policy network and fragment-based composition into a system that runs in the browser. No chat API or API key is needed, so your questions stay on your device. Hosting, optional model downloads and external links still use the network.
