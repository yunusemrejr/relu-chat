Zero-Sum Game

A game in which one player's gain exactly offsets the others' losses.

Intuition

Pure conflict — whatever one side gains the other loses.

Optimal play is max-min / min-max: maximize your guaranteed payoff against a worst-case opponent.

Worked example

Chess, Go, and Matching Pennies are zero-sum (draws counted as split).

A rock-paper-scissors tournament with {−1,0,+1}\{-1,0,+1\}{−1,0,+1} payoffs is zero-sum.
