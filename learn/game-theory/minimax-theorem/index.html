Minimax Theorem

In any finite two-player zero-sum game, max-min and min-max values coincide in mixed strategies.

Intuition

Rational worst-case planning yields the same outcome for both sides.

It guarantees an optimal defensive strategy for each player in pure conflict.

Generalizations include Sion's minimax theorem for quasi-concave/convex payoffs on compact convex sets.

Where it is used

Foundation of LP duality, robust optimization, statistical decision theory, and adversarial training of generative models.
