Replicator Dynamics

Intuition

Strategies doing better than average grow; those doing worse decline.

No rationality is assumed — the dynamics are a pure selection model.

Worked example

The math

For symmetric games, every ESS is asymptotically stable; strict Nash equilibria are asymptotically stable; interior Nash equilibria are Lyapunov-stable. Relative entropy to a Nash equilibrium is a Lyapunov function for doubly symmetric games.

Related to Lotka-Volterra equations via a nonlinear change of variables.

Where it is used

Used in population genetics, cultural learning, congestion adaptation, and multi-agent RL convergence analysis.

Models of social learning via imitation yield the same ODE.

Related topics

  • Evolutionary Game TheoryStudies strategy frequencies in large populations under selection and mutation.
  • Evolutionarily Stable StrategyA strategy that, if adopted by the population, cannot be invaded by any rare mutant.
  • Nash EquilibriumA profile of strategies where no player can profit by unilateral deviation.
  • Price of AnarchyThe worst-case ratio between the social cost of a Nash equilibrium and the social optimum.
  • Hawk-Dove GameA symmetric 2x2 conflict game used to model animal contests.
  • Evolutionarily Stable SetsA set of strategies such that every strategy in the set resists invasion by any outside mutant.
